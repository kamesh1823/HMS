Abstract

The rapid proliferation of Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) devices has created unprecedented challenges in securing sensitive medical imaging data. Traditional encryption methods often fail to balance security strength with computational efficiency in resource-constrained medical environments.

This project proposes a novel privacy-preserving framework that combines a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) Autoencoder with a Chaotic Logistic Map for medical image encryption, integrated within a full-featured Hospital Management System (HMS) built for real-world clinical deployment.

Encryption Formula
# Step 1: Derive seed from patient metadata raw = f"{patient_id}{date_of_birth}{hospital_id}" seed = SHA256(raw) → float in (0.3, 0.7) # Step 2: Generate chaotic sequence x[n+1] = r × x[n] × (1 - x[n]) # r = 3.99 key = sequence × 255 # → byte array # Step 3: XOR in latent space latent = CNN_Encoder(image) scrambled = (latent + key/255) mod 1.0 encrypted = CNN_Decoder(scrambled) # Decryption (self-inverse) restored = (scrambled - key/255) mod 1.0 original = CNN_Decoder(restored)

Conclusion

MediVault successfully demonstrates that AI-driven encryption can be seamlessly integrated into a production-grade Hospital Management System. The combination of CNN Autoencoder latent-space encryption with Chaotic Logistic Map key derivation provides strong, keyless, and computationally efficient protection for IoMT medical imaging data — while the full HMS ensures clinical usability across all hospital roles.
